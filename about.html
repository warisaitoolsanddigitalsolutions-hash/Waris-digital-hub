About Waris Digital Hub

Waris Digital Hub is a beginner-friendly directory for useful digital tools, apps, websites, games, services and digital resources.

Why it exists

The goal is to make digital discovery easier by putting practical descriptions and official provider links in one organized catalog.

How tools are organized

Tools are grouped by use such as AI, design, video, writing, productivity, communication, developer, social, entertainment, shopping, travel, websites and software.

Editorial standards

We avoid invented ratings, fake reviews, fake traffic and unsupported statistics. Tool features and pricing are described cautiously because providers can change them.

Provider links

Where an official provider destination is known, the catalog links directly to that provider. Users should verify current features, pricing, eligibility and terms before signing up or purchasing.

Updates

Listings and guides should be reviewed when important provider changes are noticed. Corrections and feedback are welcome through the contact page.

Affiliate disclosure

Some commercial links may be affiliate links. When an affiliate relationship exists, it should be identified clearly. Affiliate relationships do not justify inventing product claims.

Feedback and corrections

If you notice an outdated URL, incorrect description or other issue, contact Warisalidigitalsolutions@gmail.com with the page and the correction you believe is needed.
